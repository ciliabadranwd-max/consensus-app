Random subgraph consensus: Lk, Wk, τ and α

At every step k (k = 0 included), pick m random nodes of G so that each picked node has an edge to another picked node, take the subgraph Gk they induce, build the m × m matrices Lk and Wk = I − εLk, then compare Π τ(Wk) with τ(Π Wk).
